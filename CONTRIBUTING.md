# Contributing to Seyal

Thank you for your interest in Seyal.

## Ground rules

- Keep changes small and focused, with tests.
- Prefer the Go standard library. Explain any new dependency in your pull request.
- Never include real customer data, real names, phone numbers, email addresses or secrets in
  code, tests, seed data or commit messages. Use obviously fictional data.
- The core engine (`internal/`) must stay generic: business-specific words and logic belong in
  playbook and master-data files.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **⚠ DRAFT — NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW. Contributions from outside the project cannot be accepted
> until this section has been reviewed by a lawyer and replaced with a final CLA.**

Before your first contribution can be merged, you will be asked to agree to a Contributor
License Agreement. In short, the draft intent is:

1. You confirm that you wrote the contribution, or have the right to submit it.
2. You keep the copyright in your contribution.
3. You grant the Seyal project a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use,
   modify and distribute your contribution, including the right to relicense it, under the
   AGPL-3.0-or-later or other licences.
4. You grant a patent licence covering your contribution.

The final wording, how agreement is recorded, and whether point 3 includes relicensing rights
are all still to be decided.
